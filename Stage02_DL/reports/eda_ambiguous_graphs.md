# Stage 2 - the 5 ambiguous delay graphs (team huddle)

Generated by `Stage02_DL/02_eda_engineer.py`. Episodes are chosen by fixed rules on TRAIN + VAL hours only (never test).

## The rule (explicit, past hours only, so it can run live)

1. Remove the normal weekly pattern: subtract the TRAIN median for that hour of the week, per node.
2. Residual = that value minus its trailing 14-day median (past hours only).
3. Noise floor = robust spread of the residual on TRAIN hours (median absolute deviation x 1.4826), per node (median over nodes 7.07 h).
4. **STRUCTURAL candidate**: residual above 3 x noise floor for at least 24 hours in a row.
   **TRANSIENT shock**: above 3 x noise floor, but for fewer than 24 hours.
   **SEASONAL peak**: dwell crosses the alert line while the residual stays under 3 x noise floor (a slow rise the trailing level follows, typically shared by many nodes).
